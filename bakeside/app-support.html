Quick fixes for the usual suspects.

Make sure your internet connection is stable. Check your spam or junk folder for the email code (OTP). Then restart the app and try again.
Force-close the app and reopen it. Make sure you’re on the latest version from the App Store or Google Play. If it continues, restart your device.
Check that BakeSide has permission to access your Photos and Camera in your device settings, then try again on a stable internet connection.
