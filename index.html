Channel 4, in depth

The engine: film
one feature

Cal AI — a bootstrapped app — grew by filming one recordable feature, its photo scan, over and over. Ours is the starter / loaf photo diagnosis. We borrow the mechanism, not the curve.

See Cal AI →
The creator-native format

The creator films the Sunday bake they were making anyway. The app appears only at the moment they check the starter.

What they film

Their normal weekend bake — their phone, their kitchen. No script from us.

Where the app enters

At the starter check they photograph it and say what it tells them — 3–6 seconds, once.

Then back to baking

The tool may be in their hands; it is never the subject. Structurally incapable of being product-forward.
